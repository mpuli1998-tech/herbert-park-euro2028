Our Location · Ballsbridge, Dublin 4

Your EURO 2028 Hotel
Near Aviva Stadium.

Just a 15-minute walk from Aviva Stadium.

Stay at Herbert Park Hotel & Park Residence in Ballsbridge, Dublin 4 — approximately 1 km from Aviva Stadium and within easy walking distance of the EURO 2028 action.

Walk to the match without worrying about match-day traffic or transport queues, then return to the comfort of Herbert Park. Nearby transport makes exploring Dublin easy between matches, with the nearest bus stop around a 5-minute walk away, and Ranelagh Luas and Lansdowne Road DART stations approximately 15 minutes away on foot.

EURO 2028 Hotel & Aviva Stadium FAQs

How far is Herbert Park Hotel from Aviva Stadium?

Herbert Park Hotel & Park Residence is approximately 1 km from Aviva Stadium, making it an easy 15-minute walk through Ballsbridge for match days.

Where should I stay in Dublin for EURO 2028?

Ballsbridge is a convenient area for supporters looking for accommodation near Aviva Stadium while remaining within easy reach of Dublin city centre. Herbert Park Hotel & Park Residence provides hotel rooms and longer-stay accommodation in Dublin 4.

Does Herbert Park offer EURO 2028 hotel packages?

The EURO 2028 campaign includes planned multi-night match stays with accommodation, breakfast and supporter extras. Final package inclusions, availability and rates are subject to confirmation.

Can I stay longer and explore Dublin during EURO 2028?

Yes. Longer stays are designed to give guests time to explore Dublin, the coast and optional Ireland day-trip experiences between matches.
